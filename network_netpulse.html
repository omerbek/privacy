NetPulse privacy disclosure — updated 2026-11-18
Developer contact: omersukrubektas@gmail.com
Android application: com.network.speedpulse

NetPulse is an independent client of Cloudflare's public measurement service. After explicit start confirmation, HTTPS requests to speed.cloudflare.com transfer bounded test payloads and zero-payload latency probes. Cloudflare receives your public IP and request/network metadata and may process performance data under https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/ . NetPulse does not send an aggregate results report, account ID, location or advertising ID to the measurement endpoint. No affiliation or service availability guarantee is implied.

The 4 or 16 MiB maximum is measurement payload; HTTPS, request and response overhead are additional. Tests can affect other traffic and incur mobile data charges. They run only while foregrounded and can be stopped. HTTPS timings are not ICMP or packet-loss measurements.

Up to 100 completed results (timestamp, provider/method, budget, payload bytes, elapsed times, rates and qualifying latency medians) and cosmetic earned hearts are stored in app preferences. Named connection contexts, the selected context and an explicitly pinned result snapshot are stored separately under netpulse.baselines.v1. A context name is a label you enter, not a detected Wi-Fi name, location or network identifier. Baselines and their names remain local and are not included in measurement or advertising requests. Comparisons require the same method, provider, payload budget and compatible sample sufficiency, plus a newer result; incompatible measurements show no comparison.

Delete individual history results or clear history in the app. History deletion does not remove the separately saved baseline snapshot or context names. Pinning another result replaces that context's snapshot; no in-app context-deletion control is provided. Local history and baselines have no scheduled expiry. Clear Android app storage or remove the app and its data through the operating system to remove all local preferences, including baselines and hearts. Platform backup/transfer settings may preserve preferences according to your device settings; local deletion does not erase earlier backups or provider logs. No app-owned account or result-sync backend exists.

Google Mobile Ads may collect/share IP address, advertising/device/account identifiers, interactions and diagnostics for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention and SDK operation. Ads are requested only after UMP eligibility. Privacy choices are reachable through the shield control. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and SDK disclosure: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure . Requests use HTTPS; retention/deletion for provider data follows provider policies, not local history deletion.

The homepage banner is separate from measurement controls. Support ads are voluntary, explicitly accepted, and cosmetic only; testing is not gated on ads. No location permission, VPN, scheduled/background measurement, or device-wide traffic inspection is implemented.
