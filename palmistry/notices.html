The palm crease engine is original deterministic pixel processing, without trained weights, training photos or a third-party filter implementation. Joint landmarks only align its crop. Upper/middle/thumb-side dark-valley candidates are measured from the submitted pixels; traditional heart/head/life names are uncertain spatial labels, not verified anatomy or prediction. Shadows, ink and other marks may be mistaken for creases. Missing evidence is rejected, never invented.
