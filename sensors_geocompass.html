Privacy Policy for GeoCompass: Compass & Level

GeoCompass: Compass & Level reads hardware sensor values (magnetometer, accelerometer) directly on your device to display heading and level angles. Sensor readings remain strictly in temporary memory and are never transmitted to external servers.

1. Zero Cloud Sync

No location tracking, logging or personal identifiers are stored.

2. Advertising

Non-intrusive banner ads are provided via Google AdMob SDK.
