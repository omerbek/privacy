1. Local timer and history

FocusDial stores timer state and session history in the app's local SQLite database, focusdial.db. State includes the selected focus/break mode, running or paused state, remaining and planned duration, session identifier and start/deadline timing, cycle position, configured durations, theme, notification preference and a cosmetic supporter-heart flag. Session records contain an identifier, mode, start and end times, local completion date, planned and recorded active duration, and completed or ended-early outcome.

The database restores an interrupted timer and supplies your dated history, totals and streak. These are timer records, not a measurement of attention or productivity. The app does not upload these records to an app-operated server or attach them to advertising requests. There is no FocusDial account, app-provided cloud synchronization, or in-app history export/import service. Timer and history functions can work offline; Google advertising and consent services separately use the network.

2. Optional local notifications

If you enable completion notifications, FocusDial requests operating-system notification permission where needed and passes the session deadline to local Android alarm or iOS notification scheduling. The alert has a generic FocusDial session-completed message; it is not a server push service. Notifications may be visible on the lock screen according to your device settings. You can disable them in the app or operating-system settings, and the timer remains usable when permission is denied.

The operating system controls delivery timing, including silent, Do Not Disturb and Focus behavior. Android uses an inexact alarm and does not restore that alarm after a device reboot until the app is reopened and reconciles state. Delivery is not guaranteed; there is no continuous background service or special exact-alarm privilege.

A separate Android daily focus-start reminder is optional and off by default. Its enabled state and chosen hour/minute persist in local timer state; local delivery preferences retain the pending deadline and handled date. It uses local inexact alarms, not server push. RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED restores this reminder schedule after reboot, unlike the completion alarm above. Delivery is suppressed while the app is foregrounded, during a running session or after a completed focus session that day. Enable it with notification permission and disable it in app/device settings. Timer use does not require permission. Reminder messages may be visible on the lock screen and delivery remains subject to system restrictions.

3. Google advertising and consent services

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) may display a separate adaptive Home banner. Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) requests updated consent information and shows a form when required. Advertising initialization and requests are gated on whether the SDK permits them. Using the timer does not itself give consent to personalized advertising.

Support separately asks whether you want to watch a voluntary rewarded-interstitial, with a Not now option. All timer features remain available without watching. Only the SDK's earned-reward callback can earn the persistent cosmetic heart; closing an ad alone does not. Celebration follows both successful storage and full-screen dismissal. The heart is decorative, not money or access to timer features. No ad click is required or encouraged.

Google's SDK services may collect or share device or advertising identifiers, IP address and IP-derived approximate location, device/app information, advertising interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Consent status, region, platform and release configuration affect processing. FocusDial does not include timer state or session-history data in its ad requests. The advertising experience is not offline or a zero-collection service.

See Google's Privacy Policy, Google advertising information and the Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure. Google's policies govern its processing locations and retention. Clearing timer data does not delete records already held by Google.

4. Your choices, retention and deletion

  • The toolbar's Ad privacy control reopens Google's privacy form. Supported device settings separately provide advertising-ID controls.
  • Completion notifications can be disabled in the app or device notification settings.
  • Local timer state, settings, history and the heart remain until changed or app storage is removed; there is no automatic history-expiration period.
  • Resetting or ending a timer does not erase session history. There is no in-app individual-history or delete-all control. To remove all local records and preferences on Android, clear the app's storage in device settings. This also removes the heart and resets settings.
  • Uninstalling may remove local app data, but platform backup or restore behavior can affect what returns after reinstallation. Clear or manage system-backup copies separately.

5. Backups and security

The Android source manifest does not disable app backup or define backup-exclusion rules. Depending on your device, operating system and backup settings, system backup or device transfer may retain the database and preferences outside the installation. This is distinct from app-provided cloud synchronization. Clearing the app does not guarantee deletion of existing system backups; the relevant device or backup provider controls those copies and retention.

The database is in application storage. FocusDial does not add database encryption or provide a password-protected vault. Device access controls and operating-system protections matter, and no storage method is guaranteed secure.

6. Contact and policy changes

For privacy questions, concerns about a child's use, or requests concerning data handling, email omersukrubektas@gmail.com and identify FocusDial. Do not email your history unless you choose to disclose it. The contact cannot remotely retrieve or delete records held only on your device; use the controls above. Information voluntarily emailed is shared with the recipient and email providers to handle your request.

Changes to data handling should be reflected in this policy and the app's store disclosures. The date above identifies this draft, not a public-policy deployment.
