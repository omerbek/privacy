PDFCraft privacy policy

Updated 19 November 2026. App: PDFCraft (com.converter.pdfcraft). Privacy contact: omersukrubektas@gmail.com.

Documents and template forms

HTML, draft name and paper defaults are stored privately on this device for local recovery. Optional Blank, Letter, Meeting notes and Report form fields are escaped into generated HTML locally. The generated HTML, including any names, recipients, dates or other personal details you enter, becomes part of the same stored recovery draft. Form field mappings are not restored after reopening; continue in the HTML editor. Raw HTML cannot be converted back into form fields. Starting another form requires confirmation before replacing the current draft.

Android app backup is disabled. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes this data. Copy HTML sends content to the clipboard only at your request. Document rendering uses Android WebView with JavaScript, network loads, file access and content access disabled. Document text, form values and names are not attached to advertising requests or uploaded by PDFCraft.

Android's print screen controls Save as PDF and destination selection. PDFCraft does not receive a saved URI or confirm saving. Your selected provider may synchronize files according to its policies.

Ads and consent

Home may display a Google Mobile Ads banner after applicable UMP checks permit requests. Support separately offers a voluntary rewarded-interstitial; only the SDK reward callback earns a persistent cosmetic heart. Ads never unlock editing or printing. Privacy options are available in Support. Ads and consent use network access; the app as a whole is not offline.

Google's SDK may collect/share IP-derived approximate location, device identifiers, product interactions and diagnostics for advertising, fraud prevention and analytics under its policies. Regional consent and configuration affect processing. See Google privacy and SDK disclosure. The developer must reconcile the resolved release SDK with store disclosures before publishing.

Retention and choices

Draft and heart remain until replaced or app data is cleared. No developer account or document cloud storage is provided. Contact the developer for privacy questions. This repository policy must also be deployed to the store-linked hosted URL before release.
