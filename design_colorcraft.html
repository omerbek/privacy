Privacy Policy for ColorCraft: Palette & Contrast

ColorCraft: Palette & Contrast is a local design and developer utility. All color calculations, contrast checks, and saved palettes remain 100% on your device.

1. Zero Cloud Transmission

No user account is required and no personal color data is sent to external servers.

2. Advertising

Non-intrusive banner ads are provided via Google AdMob SDK using standard device identifiers.
