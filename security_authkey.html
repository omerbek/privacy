Privacy Policy for AuthKey: 2FA TOTP Authenticator

AuthKey: 2FA TOTP Authenticator operates as a 100% offline two-factor authentication vault. Your 2FA accounts and secret keys are stored in encrypted local memory on your device and are never transmitted to cloud servers.

1. Zero Cloud Sync

No remote servers are contacted. Verification codes are computed strictly on-device using the RFC 6238 standard.

2. Advertising

Non-intrusive banner ads are provided via Google AdMob SDK.
